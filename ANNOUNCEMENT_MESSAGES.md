### What's New

#### 🧠 Smart Content Detection
ClipboardManager now automatically recognizes emails, phone numbers, and street addresses in your clipboard items. Right-click any item to:
- Compose emails instantly
- Call numbers via FaceTime
- Open addresses in Apple Maps

No configuration needed - it just works!

#### ☁️ iCloud Sync
Your clipboard history can now sync seamlessly across all your Macs. Enable it in Settings and your clipboard items appear on every device automatically.

#### 🔄 Professional Auto-Update System
- Automatic update checking
- Beautiful notifications when updates are available
- One-click installation
- Progress tracking

#### 📸 Screenshot Management
Enable screenshot auto-copy and new screenshots automatically appear in your clipboard history. Perfect for quick sharing!

Full support for macOS Ventura, Sonoma, and future releases. We've migrated to modern APIs to ensure long-term compatibility.

Optimized for both Intel and Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3) Macs. You'll notice improved performance regardless of your Mac's processor.
